Privacy

Last updated: September 2026

Information you choose to share

This portfolio does not submit contact-form data to a server. When you use the contact form, your browser opens your configured email application with the details you entered addressed to developer@liamhuang.dev. Your email application and email provider handle that message under their own privacy practices. You can also contact Liam directly without using the form.

Site operation and hosting

The site is a static website. Its hosting provider may process standard request information, such as an IP address, request time, and requested file, in server logs to deliver and protect the site. The specific provider and its log-retention practices depend on where the site is hosted.

Third-party services

The site loads typefaces from Google Fonts. When the font stylesheet is requested, Google may receive technical connection information such as your IP address and browser details. See Google’s privacy policy for information about its handling of data.

Cookies and analytics

This site does not intentionally set analytics or advertising cookies and does not include an analytics service. Browser settings, the hosting provider, or third-party services may have their own behavior as described above.

Questions

For privacy questions, email developer@liamhuang.dev.

This page describes the site’s current implementation and is general information, not legal advice. Hosting or site changes may require updating this notice.
