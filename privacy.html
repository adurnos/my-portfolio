PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: October 6, 2026

This policy explains how Liam Huang handles personal information on liamhuang.dev. For privacy questions or requests, contact developer@liamhuang.dev.

Information you provide

If you use the contact form, you provide your name, email address, and message. If you email me directly, I receive your email address and anything you include. Please do not send sensitive personal information.

How information is used

I use your information to answer enquiries, discuss potential work, and manage related correspondence. I do not sell your personal information or use contact enquiries to subscribe you to marketing.

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, processing is based on taking steps at your request before a contract, performing a contract, legitimate interests in responding to correspondence and protecting the site, or meeting legal obligations.

Services that process information

  • FormSubmit: contact form details are sent to FormSubmit to deliver an email. Its verification flow may use Google reCAPTCHA for spam protection. FormSubmit states that it retains a submission archive for 30 days. See FormSubmit’s privacy policy (PDF, opens in a new tab).
  • Hosting: this site is configured for static hosting on Vercel. If deployed there, Vercel may process technical request information, such as IP address, requested URL, browser information, and timestamps, for delivery, security, and operational logs. See Vercel’s privacy policy (opens in a new tab).
  • External resources: Google Fonts provides the Work Sans typeface. Your browser connects to Google, which can receive technical request information, including your IP address. See Google’s privacy policy (opens in a new tab). The contact form sends form details to FormSubmit using its AJAX endpoint. Animation scripts are served locally with this site.

Service providers may process information in countries other than your own, under their applicable terms and safeguards. External links, including GitHub and project links, lead to sites with their own privacy practices.

Cookies and analytics

This site’s own code does not set cookies, use browser storage, or include advertising or analytics tracking. Third-party spam verification may use cookies or similar technologies on its own pages. Browser caching of fonts and scripts is separate from tracking.

Retention and security

I keep enquiry correspondence only for as long as needed to respond and manage related work or satisfy legal requirements. You can ask me to delete correspondence that no longer needs to be retained. Provider retention is governed by their own policies. Reasonable precautions are taken, but no internet transmission or storage method is completely secure.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or receive a copy of your personal information, and to withdraw consent where consent applies. Contact me to exercise those rights. You may also complain to your local data protection authority. I may need to verify your identity before fulfilling a request.

Children and policy changes

This portfolio is intended for professional enquiries and is not directed at children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact me so it can be addressed. Updates to this policy will appear here with a revised date.
