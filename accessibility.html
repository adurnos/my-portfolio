ACCESSIBILITY
STATEMENT

Last updated: October 6, 2026

I want this website to be usable by as many people as possible, including people who use assistive technology. Accessibility is part of the design and development process, not an optional layer.

Conformity goal

The target is the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at Level AA. This is a commitment and design goal, not a claim of certified or fully audited conformity. A comprehensive independent accessibility audit has not been completed.

Features implemented

  • Semantic headings and landmarks, a skip-to-content link, and a logical reading order.
  • Keyboard-operable links and controls with visible focus indicators.
  • Persistent contact form labels, identified required fields, native validation, and status announcements.
  • Responsive layouts and fluid typography that support browser zoom and narrow viewports.
  • Warm near-black body text, a subtle grain texture, and a whole-word About text reveal that follows reading order.
  • The film-grain texture is static and never blocks text selection or controls.
  • A native cursor during keyboard navigation, with a custom pointer for mouse input. Touch scrolling remains native. Desktop wheel scrolling glides to a stop, while keyboard scrolling, zoom gestures, and form controls retain native behavior.
  • Core content and the contact form remain available without JavaScript or if animation resources fail to load.

Known limitations

Unrevealed About words use light gray (#D9D6D2), which does not meet WCAG AA text contrast on the page background. Words switch to warm near-black in reading order as you scroll. With JavaScript disabled or in print, the full text uses the default warm near-black color.

With reduced motion enabled, scroll glide, About pinning and word reveal, entrance animations, card flips, and positional scroll animations are disabled. The About text is fully visible in warm near-black. The custom pointer uses reduced smoothing, and hover transitions are nearly instant. Visitors who are sensitive to even this limited motion may prefer disabling motion or using a browser or operating system setting that blocks animation.

The contact form continues to FormSubmit, whose spam verification and confirmation pages are controlled by a third party. Their accessibility can vary. You can bypass the form and email developer@liamhuang.dev directly.

The fixed header uses warm near-black text above page content. In forced-colors mode, the custom pointer is disabled and the fixed header follows system colors. Motion does not convey information unavailable in the text. Project previews are explicitly labeled placeholders.

Compatibility

The site uses standard HTML, CSS, and JavaScript and is intended for current versions of major browsers. Screen reader behavior depends on the browser and assistive technology combination. Browser zoom, keyboard navigation, and operating-system reduced-motion settings can be used without special site controls.

Feedback and assistance

If anything prevents you from using the site, email developer@liamhuang.dev. If possible, include the page, what you were trying to do, and the browser or assistive technology involved. You do not need to disclose a disability. I will review the issue and work toward a practical solution or provide the information another way.
