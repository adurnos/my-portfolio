SITE INFORMATION

Accessibility

Last updated: September 2026

Our commitment

I want this portfolio to be usable by as many people as possible. I am continuing to improve its accessibility. This statement describes features currently built into the site; it is not a claim of certification or full conformance with a particular standard.

Features

  • Semantic page regions, headings, and labeled navigation.
  • A skip link, visible keyboard focus, and labeled contact fields.
  • Responsive layouts for smaller screens.
  • A reduced-motion setting that disables the decorative motion effects.

Known limitations

The site has not yet received a formal accessibility audit or testing across all assistive technologies. Some decorative project previews use dense visual detail and may be harder to interpret at high zoom or with limited vision. The contact form relies on the visitor’s email application; it does not send messages directly from the website.

Feedback and assistance

If you encounter a barrier or need information in another format, email developer@liamhuang.dev. Please describe the page and issue you experienced. I will do my best to respond and provide an accessible alternative.
